🤔 Why this diagnosis?
How we calculate: LTV = ARPU × Lifespan. CAC = Marketing Spend ÷ New Customers. Ratio = LTV ÷ CAC.

Why these thresholds matter:
  • >5:1 – You're leaving growth on the table. Scale spend.
  • 3:1 – 5:1 – Healthy and scalable. Focus on retention to push higher.
  • 1.5:1 – 3:1 – Marginal. Tighten ICP and improve onboarding.
  • <1.5:1 – Critical. Stop spending. Fix your funnel and ICP.

What is a healthy LTV:CAC ratio?
3:1 or above is healthy. Above 5:1 may mean you are under-investing in growth. Below 1.5:1 signals a structural problem requiring immediate attention to ICP, funnel architecture, or retention strategy.
What does a Fractional CMO actually do?
At NanoMunch, a fractional CMO provides 15–30 hours per month of senior marketing leadership — GTM strategy, team direction, funnel architecture, and the actual build of systems your team can operate without us.
How quickly can you build an AI growth system?
A GTM Sprint delivers a complete system in 4 weeks. An AI Growth System build — CRM automation, email sequences, lead scoring, analytics — typically takes 3–6 weeks depending on scope.
Do you work with companies outside the US?
Yes. Our clients span US, UK, EU, Canada, Australia, and beyond. We are a global operation. We also specialise in pan-African market entry for international companies expanding into African markets.
Can I embed your tools on my own site?
Yes. We build white-label versions of all our interactive tools for businesses wanting branded calculators or lead capture tools on their own domains. Enquire via email for availability.
What is your CPA marketing track record?
We achieved a 35% conversion rate across CPA campaigns within 90 days — 7 to 17 times above the 2–5% industry benchmark. CPA marketing design and management is a core NanoMunch capability.
