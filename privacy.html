Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

NanoMunch (Pty) Ltd ("NanoMunch," "we," "us") operates nanomunch.com and its associated tools (the LTV:CAC Calculator, GTM Quick Scan, Pricing Sensitivity Calculator, and related diagnostics). This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what rights you have over it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use one of our free tools or book a call, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, and phone number (if you submit one of our lead forms)
  • The answers you give within a diagnostic tool (e.g., GTM Quick Scan responses, pricing inputs) — used to generate your results
  • Standard usage data (pages visited, general location by country, device/browser type) via analytics

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate and deliver the results of the tool you used
  • To follow up with you if you've requested a call or more information
  • To understand, in aggregate, how our tools are used so we can improve them
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties

3. Cookies

We use cookies for basic analytics (to understand site traffic) and to remember that you've acknowledged our cookie notice. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the site will still function, though some preferences won't be remembered between visits.

4. Data Sharing

We use a small number of third-party service providers to operate our tools and communications (for example, scheduling software for booking calls, and email delivery infrastructure for following up on form submissions). These providers only receive the information needed to perform their specific function and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you share with us. No online system is 100% secure, but we do not store sensitive information (such as payment details) on our own servers.

6. Your Rights

Wherever you're located, you can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing us using the details below. If you're in the European Union or UK, this reflects your rights under GDPR; if you're in South Africa, this reflects your rights under POPIA.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: pey@nanomunch.com

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
